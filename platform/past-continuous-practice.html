A Rainy Afternoon

Reading 1 · Past Continuous

🎧 استمع إلى الفقرة الأولى – A Rainy Afternoon

📖 Reading 1 – A Rainy Afternoon

Pre-Intermediate
Last Saturday afternoon, I was sitting in the living room and reading a book. The weather was changing, and it was raining heavily outside. My mother was preparing lunch in the kitchen, while my younger brother was playing with his toys. My father was watching the news on television.

Suddenly, we heard a loud noise outside. Everyone stopped what they were doing and looked through the window. A large tree was falling near our garden because of the strong wind. Luckily, nobody was hurt. While my father was checking the garden, my mother was calling our neighbour to make sure everything was okay.

After the rain stopped, we went outside and looked at the fallen tree. It was a surprising afternoon, but we were all safe.

📝 أسئلة الفقرة الأولى

🎯 اختر الإجابة الصحيحة.

What was the writer doing last Saturday afternoon?

🎯 اختر الإجابة الصحيحة.

What happened outside?

✍️ لاحظ الزمن المستخدم في الجملة.

My mother was preparing lunch.

ما التركيب الصحيح الذي تكوّن منه الفعل؟

✍️ أجب بجملة كاملة.

What was your family doing when the tree was falling?

An Unexpected Meeting

Reading 2 · Past Continuous

🎧 استمع إلى الفقرة الثانية – An Unexpected Meeting

📖 Reading 2 – An Unexpected Meeting

Pre-Intermediate
Yesterday, I was walking through the park after school when I saw an old friend. His name was Adam, and I hadn't seen him for almost two years. He was sitting on a bench and listening to music. When he noticed me, he smiled and took off his headphones.

We started talking about our lives. Adam told me that he was studying at a new school. He was also learning how to play the guitar, and he was practising every evening. I told him that I was taking English lessons and trying to improve my speaking skills.

While we were talking, a little boy ran towards us. He was looking for his dog, which was running around the park. Adam helped him find it behind a tree. The boy thanked us and ran home happily.

It was a lovely meeting, and we decided to meet again the following weekend.

📝 أسئلة الفقرة الثانية

🎯 اختر الإجابة الصحيحة.

Where did the writer meet Adam?

🎯 اختر الإجابة الصحيحة.

What was Adam learning to play?

✍️ أكمل الجملة بالفعل الصحيح.

While we ______ talking, a little boy ran towards us.

✍️ أجب بجملة كاملة.

What was the little boy looking for?

A Busy Day at the School

Reading 3 · Past Continuous

🎧 استمع إلى الفقرة الثالثة – A Busy Day at the School

📖 Reading 3 – A Busy Day at the School

Pre-Intermediate
Last Thursday, our school was preparing for its annual science exhibition. Students were working in different classrooms, and teachers were helping them with their projects. Some students were building small robots, while others were making posters about the environment.

My classmates and I were working on a project about clean energy. I was writing the information for our poster, while Sara was preparing a small model of a solar panel. Our friend Omar was searching for pictures and useful facts on the internet.

Everything was going well until the electricity suddenly went off. Some students were using computers, so they couldn't continue their work. Our teacher asked everyone to stay calm. While we were waiting for the electricity to return, we discussed our ideas and planned the final details of our project.

Fortunately, the electricity came back after twenty minutes, and we finished our work before the end of the day. The exhibition was a great success!

📝 أسئلة الفقرة الثالثة

🎯 اختر الإجابة الصحيحة.

What was the school preparing for?

🎯 اختر الإجابة الصحيحة.

What happened to the electricity?

✍️ اختر الزمن الصحيح.

The students ______ working in different classrooms.

✍️ أجب بجملة كاملة.
