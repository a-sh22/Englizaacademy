📖 قراءة | In • On • At

ستقرأ أربعة نصوص قصيرة، وستشاهد كيف تُستخدم كلمات in و on و at داخل مواقف حقيقية، ثم تجيب عن أسئلة بسيطة لفهم النص.

💡 نصيحة صغيرة

لا تتوقف عند كل كلمة لا تعرفها.

حاول فهم معنى الجملة كاملة، ثم لاحظ كيف استُخدمت in، on و at داخلها.

📖 Reading 1 – My School Day

Adam is a student.

He wakes up at 6:30 every morning.

He studies English in the morning before school.

He goes to school on Sunday, Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday.

His classes start at 8:00.

Today, he is sitting on the bus and talking to his friend.

His school bag is in the bus, and he is happy because he likes school.

📝 أسئلة الفقرة

🎯 اختر الإجابة الصحيحة.

What time do Adam's classes start?

✍️ أكمل الفراغ.

Adam studies English _____ the morning.

✍️ أجب عن السؤال.

Is Adam sitting on the bus?

🎯 اختر الإجابة الصحيحة.

Where is Adam's school bag?

📖 Reading 2 – A Family Trip

Today is Friday.

The Brown family is going to the beach.

They are in the car at 9:00 in the morning.

The children are talking and laughing in the car.

They arrive at the beach and sit on the sand.

Now the children are playing in the water, and their parents are watching them.

The family is very happy because they enjoy spending time together.

📝 أسئلة الفقرة

🎯 اختر الإجابة الصحيحة.

Where is the family at 9:00?

✍️ أكمل الفراغ.

They sit _____ the sand.

✍️ أجب عن السؤال.

Are the children playing in the water?

Where are Mona and her sister?

Their bags are _____ the table.

Where are their phones?
