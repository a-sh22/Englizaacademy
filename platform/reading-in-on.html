📖 Reading Practice – In • On • At

في هذا التمرين ستقرأ فقرات قصيرة وتراجع استخدام In, On, و At في الجمل.

📖 قبل أن تبدأ القراءة

ستلاحظ أن بعض الكلمات تحتها خط.

اضغط على أي كلمة منها، وسيظهر لك معناها، ونوعها، ومثال عليها، مع ترجمتها وطريقة نطقها.

استخدم هذه الميزة إذا واجهت كلمة جديدة، ثم أكمل القراءة وحاول فهم الفقرة.

📖 Reading 1 – My School Day

Adam is a student. He wakes up at 6:30 every morning. He studies English in the morning before school. He goes to school on Sunday, Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday. His classes start at 8:00. Today, he is sitting on the bus and talking to his friend. His school bag is in the bus, and he is happy because he likes school.

📝 أسئلة الفقرة

🎯 اختر الإجابة الصحيحة.

What time do Adam's classes start?

✍️ أكمل الفراغ.

Adam studies English _ the morning.

✍️ أجب عن السؤال.

Is Adam sitting on the bus?

🎯 اختر الإجابة الصحيحة.

Where is Adam's school bag?

📖 Reading 2 – A Family Trip

Today is Friday. The Brown family is going to the beach. They are in the car at 9:00 in the morning. The children are talking and laughing in the car. They arrive at the beach and sit on the sand. Now the children are playing in the water, and their parents are watching them. The family is very happy because they enjoy spending time together.

📝 أسئلة الفقرة

🎯 اختر الإجابة الصحيحة.

Where is the family at 9:00?

✍️ أكمل الفراغ.

They sit _ the sand.

✍️ أجب عن السؤال.

Are the children playing in the water?

📖 Reading 3 – At the Library

Mona likes reading books. Every Saturday, she goes to the library with her sister. Today, they are at the library at 10:00 in the morning. Mona is sitting on a chair and reading an English book. Her sister is looking for a new book in the history section. Their bags are on the table, and their phones are in the bags. They are happy because they love reading together.

📝 أسئلة الفقرة

🎯 اختر الإجابة الصحيحة.

Where are Mona and her sister today?

✍️ أكمل الفراغ.

Their bags are _ the table.

✍️ أجب عن السؤال.

Are their phones in the bags?
