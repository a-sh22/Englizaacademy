📖 قراءة | Present Simple

هنا ستتدرب على المضارع البسيط من خلال قراءة نصوص و الاجابة على أسئلتها

✨ نصيحة قبل أن تبدأ

لا تحاول ترجمة كل كلمة تقرؤها.

اقرأ الفقرة كاملة أولًا، ثم حاول فهم الفكرة العامة.

إذا وجدت كلمة جديدة، فلا تقلق. حاول معرفة معناها من خلال الجملة، ثم أجب عن الأسئلة.

📖 Reading 1 – Daily Routine

Ahmed wakes up at 7:00 every morning. He brushes his teeth and washes his face. Then he eats breakfast with his family. He drinks tea, but he doesn't drink coffee. Does he drive to work? No, he doesn't. He goes to work by bus at 8:00. He comes home at 5:00 and watches TV in the evening.

📝 أسئلة الفقرة

🎯 اختر الإجابة الصحيحة.

What time does Ahmed wake up?

🎯 اختر الإجابة الصحيحة.

How does Ahmed go to work?

✍️ حوّل الجملة إلى النفي.

Ahmed drinks tea.

✍️ حوّل الجملة إلى سؤال.

Ahmed watches TV in the evening.

✍️ أجب عن السؤال.

Does Ahmed drink coffee?

📖 Reading 2 – My Family

Sara lives with her family in a small house. She has one brother and two sisters. Her father works in a hospital. Her mother teaches English at a school. Does Sara have a pet? Yes, she does. She has a small cat, and she loves it very much. Every evening, the family eats dinner together.

📝 أسئلة الفقرة

🎯 اختر الإجابة الصحيحة.

Where does Sara's father work?

🎯 اختر الإجابة الصحيحة.

Does Sara have a pet?

✍️ حوّل الجملة إلى النفي.

Sara has a small cat.

✍️ حوّل الجملة إلى سؤال.

Her mother teaches English.

✍️ أجب عن السؤال.

Does the family eat dinner together?

📖 Reading 3 – Hobbies

Omar likes sports very much. He plays football every Friday with his friends. He also likes reading books in the evening. He doesn't watch TV every day. Does he like swimming? Yes, he does. Sometimes he swims with his brother at the beach. Omar enjoys his free time because he always does something fun.

📝 أسئلة الفقرة

🎯 اختر الإجابة الصحيحة.

When does Omar play football?

🎯 اختر الإجابة الصحيحة.

Does Omar watch TV every day?

✍️ حوّل الجملة إلى النفي.

Omar likes sports.

✍️ حوّل الجملة إلى سؤال.

Omar reads books in the evening.

✍️ أجب عن السؤال.

Does Omar like swimming?

📖 Reading 4 – School

Ali is a student at a middle school. He goes to school from Sunday to Thursday. His favorite subject is English because he likes learning new words. He doesn't arrive late for school. Does he do his homework every day? Yes, he does. After school, he studies for one hour and then plays with his friends. Ali enjoys school because he learns something new every day.
