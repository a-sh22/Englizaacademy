My Last Weekend

Reading 1 · Past Simple

🎧استمع إلى الفقرة الأولى – My Last Weekend

📖 Reading 1 – My Last Weekend

Past Simple
Last weekend, I visited my grandmother with my family. We arrived at her house in the morning. She welcomed us and prepared a delicious lunch. After lunch, my brother and I played in the garden. We also took some pictures together. In the evening, we watched a funny movie and talked about our week. I didn't want to leave because I enjoyed every moment. It was a wonderful weekend.

📝 أسئلة الفقرة الأولى

🎯 اختر الإجابة الصحيحة.

Where did the writer go last weekend?

🎯 اختر الإجابة الصحيحة.

What did they do in the evening?

✍️ حوّل الجملة إلى النفي.

She prepared a delicious lunch.

A Day at the Beach

Reading 2 · Past Simple

🎧استمع إلى الفقرة الثانية – A Day at the Beach

📖 Reading 2 – A Day at the Beach

Past Simple
Yesterday, my friends and I went to the beach. We left home early and arrived at the beach at nine o'clock. The weather was sunny, so we decided to swim. My friend Ahmed brought some sandwiches and cold drinks. We ate our lunch under a big tree. Then, we collected beautiful shells and took photos of the sea. Did we enjoy our day? Of course, we did! We returned home in the afternoon, and I felt very happy.

🎯 اختر الإجابة الصحيحة.

What did they collect?

✍️ حوّل الجملة إلى النفي.

We collected beautiful shells.

The Day I Lost My Bag

Reading 3 · Past Simple

🎧استمع إلى الفقرة الثالثة – The Day I Lost My Bag

📖 Reading 3 – The Day I Lost My Bag

Past Simple
Last Monday, I went shopping with my sister. We visited several stores and bought some clothes. When we finished shopping, I couldn't find my bag. I looked inside the car, but it wasn't there. I felt worried because my phone and money were inside it. My sister asked a shop assistant for help. Luckily, a woman found my bag near the entrance and gave it to the security guard. I thanked her and felt relieved. It was a stressful day, but everything ended well.

📝 أسئلة الفقرة الثالثة

🎯 اختر الإجابة الصحيحة.

Who did the writer go shopping with?

🎯 اختر الإجابة الصحيحة.

Where did the woman find the bag?

✍️ حوّل الجملة إلى النفي.

The woman found my bag.

My First School Trip

Reading 4 · Past Simple

🎧استمع إلى الفقرة الرابعة – My First School Trip

📖 Reading 4 – My First School Trip

Past Simple
Two years ago, my school organized a trip to a science museum. All the students arrived at school early in the morning. We travelled by bus with our teachers. At the museum, we saw interesting experiments and learned about space and technology. Our guide explained how some machines worked, and we asked many questions. After the tour, we ate lunch together and bought small gifts from the museum shop. Before we left, our teacher took a group photo. I learned many new things that day, and I never forgot that trip.

Where did the students go?

🎯 اختر الإجابة الصحيحة.

How did they travel?

✍️ حوّل الجملة إلى النفي.

Our school organized a trip.
