READING 01

A New Chapter in My Life

B1
🎧 Listen to the passage

Over the past few months, my life has changed in many ways. I have started learning a new language, and I have discovered that learning something new can be both challenging and exciting.

At first, I didn't make much progress. I have made several mistakes, and sometimes I have felt like giving up. However, I haven't stopped trying. I have already learned many useful expressions, and I have recently started watching English videos without subtitles.

My best friend has encouraged me since I began this journey. She has also joined an online English course, and we have practiced speaking together several times.

Have I become fluent yet? Not really, but I have improved a lot. I haven't reached my final goal, but I know I am getting closer every day.

READING 02

The Town That Has Changed

B1
🎧 Listen to the passage

My hometown has changed significantly over the last ten years. Many new buildings have appeared, and several old streets have become wider and more modern.

The local authorities have built new parks and created more public spaces for families. They have also improved the transportation system, so people can travel around the town more easily.

However, not everything has improved. Traffic has increased, and air pollution has become a serious problem in some areas. Some residents have complained about the noise, but the town hasn't found a complete solution yet.

Have these changes made life better? In many ways, yes. The town has become more comfortable, and people have more places to visit. Recently, the local council has announced new environmental projects. Hopefully, these projects will make the town a healthier place for future generations.

READING 03

Have You Ever Had an Unexpected Experience?

B1
🎧 Listen to the passage

Have you ever experienced something completely unexpected? I have, and it has become one of my favourite memories.

A few months ago, I received a message from an old friend I hadn't spoken to for years. She told me that she was visiting my city, and I was so excited because I hadn't seen her since we graduated from school.

We have kept in touch occasionally, but we haven't met in person for a long time. When she arrived, I decided to organize a small surprise for her. I invited some of our old classmates, prepared her favourite food, and decorated the room with photographs from our school days.

She has never expected anything like that! When she walked into the room, she was completely surprised. We have taken many pictures, shared old stories, and laughed about unforgettable moments.

I have realized that even a simple meeting can create beautiful memories. Since that day, we have promised to meet more often.
