📖 قراءة | Present Simple

هنا ستتدرب على المضارع البسيط من خلال قراءة نصوص و الاجابة على أسئلتها

📖 قبل أن تبدأ القراءة

ستلاحظ أن بعض الكلمات تحتها خط.

اضغط على أي كلمة منها، وسيظهر لك معناها، ونوعها، ومثال عليها، مع ترجمتها وطريقة نطقها.

استخدم هذه الميزة إذا واجهت كلمة جديدة، ثم أكمل القراءة وحاول فهم الفقرة.

📖 Reading 1 – Daily Routine

Ahmed is walking in the park now. He is wearing a blue T-shirt and black shoes. His friends are playing football near the trees. Ahmed is not playing football because he is tired. Is he sitting on a bench? No, he isn't. He is drinking water and talking with his friends. Everyone is having a good time in the park.

📝 أسئلة الفقرة

🎯 اختر الإجابة الصحيحة.

Where is Ahmed walking?

🎯 اختر الإجابة الصحيحة.

Is Ahmed playing football?

✍️ حوّل الجملة إلى النفي.

Ahmed is wearing a blue T-shirt.



📖 Reading 2 – My Family

Sara is helping her mother in the kitchen. Her mother is cooking dinner for the family. Sara is washing the vegetables carefully. Her little brother is not helping because he is doing his homework. Is her father watching TV? No, he isn't. He is setting the table for dinner. Everyone is working together to prepare a nice meal.

📝 أسئلة الفقرة

🎯 اختر الإجابة الصحيحة.

Where is Sara helping her mother?

🎯 اختر الإجابة الصحيحة.

Is Sara's father watching TV?

✍️ حوّل الجملة إلى النفي.

Sara is washing the vegetables.



📖 Reading 3 – Hobbies

The students are sitting in the classroom now. The teacher is explaining a new English lesson. Some students are writing in their notebooks. One student is not listening because he is looking out of the window. Is the teacher using the computer? Yes, she is. She is showing pictures to the class. Everyone is learning something new today.

📝 أسئلة الفقرة

🎯 اختر الإجابة الصحيحة.

What is the teacher explaining?

🎯 اختر الإجابة الصحيحة.

Is the teacher using the computer?

✍️ حوّل الجملة إلى النفي.

The students are writing in their notebooks.
